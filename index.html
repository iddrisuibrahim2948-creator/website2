About AZ MotionCare

AZ MotionCare Physiotherapy helps people recover from injuries, stroke, surgery and mobility challenges.

Every person arrives with a different story: a back that won't settle, a shoulder that stopped cooperating, a recovery that feels slow. Our work starts with listening to that story, then building a rehabilitation approach around it, using evidence-based physiotherapy.

The aim is practical. We want to help you regain movement, support your recovery, and make everyday life easier, with care that treats you as a person and not just a diagnosis.

Rehabilitation works best when it fits the person doing it.

  • Personalised rehabilitation
  • Evidence-based physiotherapy
  • Support for recovery and regaining movement
  • Improved mobility and quality of life
  • Compassionate, respectful patient care

    Home physiotherapy

    For many people, getting to a clinic is part of the challenge. Where appropriate, you may be able to receive physiotherapy support in the comfort of your own home, with exercises and guidance that fit the space you actually live in.

    Not every condition can be treated at home. We will talk through your situation and let you know what is suitable.

    Ask About Home Physiotherapy

    Telerehabilitation

    Virtual physiotherapy can offer remote guidance and follow-up where appropriate, so you can keep working on your recovery without travelling.

    • Guidance on your exercises from home
    • Follow-up between in-person sessions
    • Useful when travel is difficult

    Virtual care is not right for every situation. We will advise whether it suits your needs.
